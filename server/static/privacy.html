Tinker is a physics puzzle game. You don't create an account, and we don't ask for your email, phone number or real name. This page explains what the game stores, why, and how to have it deleted.

What we collect

DataWhy
A random device id created by the game (not your phone's hardware id)To keep your coins, scores and progress, and to stop cheating
The name you choose to show on leaderboards (optional) and your country (from your device language settings)Leaderboards
Gameplay: your Daily solutions, scores, levels solved, levels you share from the SandboxLeaderboards, checking solutions, sharing
Coins, purchases and their status (we never see your card or bank details; payments go through Google Play, the App Store or Stripe)To deliver what you bought, refunds, accounting
Device and app information: platform, operating system version, device model, browser, app version, when the game was openFixing bugs, supporting devices, counting active players
A push notification token (only if you allow notifications), and whether a notification was received or openedDaily reminders and game news; measuring which messages help

Ads

The game may show ads (for example to earn a free try or coins). When an ad network is used (Google AdMob in the app, Google H5 Games Ads in the browser), that network may collect device identifiers such as the advertising ID, your IP address and ad interactions under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can reset or limit the advertising ID in your device settings. A "no ads" pass removes ads between levels.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't use your location beyond the country of your device language settings.
  • We don't access your contacts, photos, microphone or files.

Where it is stored

On Google Cloud (Firestore and Cloud Run, region europe-west1), protected in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Service providers: Google Cloud and Firebase (hosting, notifications), Google Play / Apple / Stripe (payments), and the ad network you see ads from.

How long

As long as you play. Ledger entries for purchases are kept as long as accounting law requires. Inactive data (no use for 24 months) is deleted.

Your choices and deletion

Turn off notifications in Settings or in your phone settings. Delete your data yourself at any time: Settings → "Delete my data" (tap twice). It removes your coins, scores, progress, leaderboard entries and device information from our servers at once; purchase records are kept only as anonymous accounting lines. You can also write to {{CONTACT}} with your device id (Settings → tap "Device id" to copy it); we delete within 30 days. Uninstalling the game removes the data stored on your device.

Children

Tinker is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used the game, contact us and we'll delete the data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the game shows a note.
